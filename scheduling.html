What runs behind it

Read

The AI turns faxes, e-referrals and call notes into structured fields: patient, reason, specialty, urgency and insurance. Anything below 85% confidence goes to a person.

Match

It ranks open slots across all sites by clinical fit, wait, travel time and the patient’s stated preferences. It never uses payer type to rank patients.

Confirm

The agent approves in one click. The patient gets a text with options, reminders at 7 days and 48 hours, and a one-tap way to reschedule.

Close the loop

The referring doctor sees every status change, and the visit summary is sent from the EHR within 72 hours of the appointment.

Learn

Cancellations are backfilled from the waitlist, and every booking, override and no-show feeds the analytics dashboard.
