Where the $69M margin gap comes from

Bellwood earns 4% on $2.3B against a 7% board target. Care quality is not the issue: 82% of referring physicians rate it positively. The gap sits in labour cost, manual admin work and referrals lost before the first visit.

Wait times are a routing problem
